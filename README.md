## 🧪 Our Story

Founded on **November 8, 2026**, **LLAStudio** (*LemonLimeAcid*) was born from a simple mission: crafting sharp, fast, and bloat-free developer tools.

While modern software often grows heavy and overly complex, our solo lab embraces an artisanal, experimental philosophy. Every project is engineered for maximum efficiency—combining the raw speed of low-level languages (**Rust**) with the fluidity of modern interactive interfaces (**TypeScript / JavaScript**).

### The Lemon, Lime & Acid Philosophy

Our ecosystem is built around three distinct lines:

* 🍋 **Lemon — Fast Micro-Utilities:** Lightweight, single-purpose CLI and system tools designed to run instantaneously without weighing down your workflow.
* 🍋🟩 **Lime — Visual Studios & Workflows:** Interactive environments and intelligent agents built to design, visualize, and automate development pipelines.
* 🧪 **Acid — R&D & Experiments:** The lab's playground for testing novel architectures, protocols, and cutting-edge concepts.
